# Changelog

All notable Primary Skill Evolution changes.

## [0.2.0]

### Added

- Settings in Game settings > Mods tab:
  - switch off any of the mod's mechanics: magazine and reload, Perfect Reload, aim at cursor, charge window, cleaver catch, stamina, finisher and Rage, Lunge and Launcher. Online, the host's settings apply to everyone;
  - move the stamina and rage meters to any corner or to a spot of your own;
  - hide the series rank and the screen effects;
  - draw the reload bar, the stamina window and Pierce's charge as a ring around the cursor;
  - reset everything to default.
- Settings are kept between sessions.
- Skills++ (optional): each level of a gunner's primary adds a tenth to its magazine, at least one round, and widens the reload window by 2% of the bar, up to 40%.
- Skills++ (optional): each level of a melee primary adds 10% stamina and makes Rage last 10% longer.
- Sniper: a perfect reload of Snipe also gives Perfect Reload, once every 7 seconds.

### Changed

- Sniper's Snipe aims almost straight up and down: ±85° instead of ±20°.
- Stamina is spent only by strikes, Miner's thrown pickaxes, lunges and launchers that hit an enemy. Swinging at the air no longer brings up the refill window.
- Miner's thrown pickaxes fill the finisher bar when they hit, not when thrown.
- Rage has its own buff icon: the gem from the rage meter.

## [0.1.1]

### Changed

- Bandit's Blast: the magazine holds 8 rounds instead of 6.
- Breathless now weakens only the primary: its strikes deal half damage. Other skills, Lunge and Launcher keep their full damage. It used to be -30% attack speed and -30% damage on everything.
- Lunge: you can't be hurt for a third of a second instead of a full second.
- Mercenary's signature: each cut deals 75% instead of 150%, the final blow 300% instead of 500%.
- HAN-D's signature: 6 drones instead of 2.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Reworked primary attacks (Z) of the vanilla survivors: gunners, special primaries and melee.
- The Action key (R by default). Rebind it in Game settings > Mods tab.
- Gunners (Commando, Bandit, Enforcer, Pilot, Robomando, Engineer): magazines and an active reload. Hit the reload window for Perfect Reload: +25% attack speed, +10% movement speed and 4% lifesteal for 7 seconds.
- Mouse aim: with the vanilla mouse aim option on, gunner primaries, Sniper's Snipe and Enforcer's Shrapnel Grenade fly toward the cursor.
- Huntress's Strafe: a mark on the Action key, homing arrows through thin walls, every third hit on one target crits and ricochets.
- Huntress's Pierce and Loader's alternative primary: a window on the charge bar. Release inside it for a full charge and Steady Hand: +30% attack speed.
- CHEF's Dice: catch the returning cleaver to sharpen the next throw. A caught MINCE is thrown again for free.
- Artificer's Flame Chakrams: the chakram flies to the cursor; Action sets a ring of four burning chakrams around her.
- Melee (Acrid, HAN-D, Miner, Mercenary, Loader, Drifter and the melee alternatives of Bandit, Sniper and CHEF):
  - Stamina with a refill window and two buffs: Breathless and Second Wind.
  - Finisher: a full bar sets off an explosion, the hero's own signature effect and Rage.
  - Series: a style rank from D to SSS.
  - Lunge in the air and Launcher on the ground, both on the Action key.
- English and Russian localization.
- Multiplayer support.
